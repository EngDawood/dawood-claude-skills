# Check details

## Per format

| Check | docx | pptx | xlsx | pdf |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| comments | `word/comments*.xml`, comment anchors removed | `ppt/comments/*`, comment authors | legacy notes, threaded comments, persons | Text, Highlight, FreeText, Stamp, Popup and other non-link annotations |
| tracked changes | insert, delete, move, formatting revisions; track-changes switch turned off | n/a | n/a | n/a |
| speaker notes | n/a | `ppt/notesSlides/*` | n/a | n/a |
| metadata | creator, lastModifiedBy, description, Company, Manager, custom.xml, thumbnail, tool name in Application | same | same | Author, Creator, Producer, Subject, Keywords, XMP stream |
| hidden | `w:vanish` text | hidden slides | hidden and very hidden sheets | n/a |
| format | Arabic paragraphs without RTL, 3+ empty paragraphs in a row, more than 3 direct fonts, missing relationship targets | empty slides, empty title or body placeholders, Arabic paragraphs without RTL | error cells, formulas without a stored value, external workbook links | pages without extractable text, encrypted file |

## AI-style heuristics

Rules adapted from the humanizer skill (blader, MIT). They are regex heuristics, so every finding is `manual` and needs a human read.

- One sighting flags a paragraph: not-X-but-Y contrasts (English and Arabic forms), stock closers, deep-sounding sayings, staged run-ups, arguments with no one, em or spaced dashes, inflated or sales wording, strong stock words (delve, tapestry, pivotal, underscore, showcase), -ing riders, unnamed authorities, knowledge-limit hedges, emoji and arrows.
- Two different words flag a paragraph: common words that are honest on their own (additionally, crucial, enhance, robust, landscape, valuable, "serves as", "plays a key role", and a few Arabic stock phrases).
- docx only: three or more paragraphs that open with a bold label ending in a colon.
- Not detected: forced triads, repeated sentence openings, stacked qualifiers, hidden actors. `references/humanizer-rules.md` lists them for the rewrite pass.
- Quoted text is not skipped. Ignore a finding that sits inside a quotation.

## Citation heuristics

Recognised forms: parenthetical `(Smith, 2020)`, `(Smith & Lee, 2020, p. 4)`, `(الرويلي، 2020م)`, multi-source `(A, 2019; B, 2021)`, numeric `[3]`, `[1-3]`, and narrative `Smith (2020)`, `الرويلي (2020)`.

- Dense paragraph: more than `--max-per-paragraph` citations (default 3). The default is a working heuristic, not a style rule. Raise it for literature-review sections.
- Repeated citation: the same parenthetical or numeric citation twice in one paragraph. `clean --thin-citations` (docx only) keeps the first and removes later copies together with the space before them.
- Narrative citations are never removed. A citation split across runs is skipped, so a few repeats can survive and show up in the re-scan.
- pptx, xlsx and pdf report citations only. Editing them is a manual step.

## Fill-in fields

- A field is square brackets holding 2 to 40 characters with at least two letters in a row and no digit: `[Full name]`, `[Student ID]`, `[اسم الطالب]`.
- Left alone: brackets with a digit (`[3]`, `[البقرة: 255]`, `[0, 1]`), single-letter content (`[a, b]`), `[sic]`, missing-source markers and `[Insert ...]` / to-do markers, which stay `manual` placeholders.
- `clean` removes the field, a separator and bare label in front of it (`— ID [Student ID]`), and trailing separators. A label ending in a colon stays. `--keep-fields` turns this off.
- Works inside one text node. A field split across runs survives and shows in the re-scan; blank it by hand.

## Notes, tool marks and traps

- Notes: English and Arabic phrases addressed to a writer or student ("note to student", "replace this with your", "you should add", "before submitting", "ملاحظة للطالب", "يرجى استبدال", "قبل التسليم", "ضع اسمك"). Body text, headers, footers, footnotes and slides are scanned.
- Tool marks: "generated by ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini / AI / Gamma / Canva", chatbot domains, "تم إنشاؤه بواسطة".
- Traps: each `--find WORD` is matched case-insensitively in every paragraph.

## Slide footers

Text in `ftr` and `dt` placeholders on slides is reported; `clean` removes those shapes. Slide numbers stay.

## Reference list

The last heading that reads References, Bibliography, Works cited, Sources, المراجع, قائمة المراجع, المصادر or المصادر والمراجع starts the list. Entries are later paragraphs longer than 15 characters; an entry counts as Arabic when at least half its letters are Arabic.

## Clean behaviour worth knowing

- Tracked changes are accepted: insertions stay, deletions go. A deleted paragraph mark is dropped without merging the two paragraphs. A tracked-deleted table row is removed.
- Legacy comment drawings (`vmlDrawing`) of a sheet are removed only when that sheet has notes.
- Hidden content, to-do placeholders, notes and residue are reported, never deleted by the script.
- `--strip-citations` (docx) removes parenthetical and numeric citations from body paragraphs before the reference list. Paragraphs that open with a numeric label such as `[1]` keep that label. Narrative citations such as `Smith (2020)` stay and need a manual rewrite.
- `docProps/app.xml` Application is blanked only when it names a known tool (python-docx, openpyxl, LibreOffice and similar).
- Original files are never overwritten. Output defaults to `<name>.clean.<ext>`.
- PDF: annotations other than links and form widgets are removed. Text is not touched.

## Exit codes

`scan` exits 1 when any finding exists. `clean` exits 1 when auto-fixable findings remain after cleaning.
